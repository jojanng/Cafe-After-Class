# A quiet neighborhood café

Café After Class is an original, unbranded contemporary Korean neighborhood café in miniature. Café Noon in Brossard informs the palette of honey wood, warm ribbed pendants, neutral plaster, charcoal joinery and window stools. It is not a replica of its floor plan, branding, photos or menu. The Roost informs intimacy, Chibi-Robo tactile proportions, and Spirit City a quiet mood; no external assets are used.

## Fixed interior

Compose a fixed, slightly elevated three-quarter customer view near the service counter. Use rounded forms, honey and oat tones, charcoal, muted olive and restrained metallic highlights. Two pendants, a plaster rear wall, window stools, and a few original still-life prints give the room context; avoid clutter. Mobile keeps the same viewpoint and materials with a tighter crop and the accessible interactive paper slip below the room.

Staff stand behind the counter. The espresso machine's controls, portafilters and drip bay face them, while the viewer sees the outside and end cap of the machine. The counter does not turn. The guest pickup, served glass and projected paper receipt have independent room-space anchors on the customer edge, not within the machine group. Old decoration never changes this composition.

## Calm and responsive service

The study view stays still; the HTML timer updates once per second, without running a continuous scene loop. Motion is a response to a saved action: a 380 ms manual receipt pickup, 420 ms ingredient packet arrival, 700 ms stationary pour and fill, and 560 ms guest pickup slide. Visibility sync, reload and resize reconstruct the saved state rather than replay an effect. Reduced motion shows the final pose immediately.

The focus slip accepts an explicit goal and a 25- or 45-minute timer. On expiry the user can collect a receipt and earn a flavor or carry the goal without a reward. The counter takes one order at a time, with a named recipe, possible free swap or return to study. Make consumes ingredients; Serve learns the drink and advances the order. Menu, Pantry, Receipts and the separate 10-second demo remain. No editor, exterior, camera movement, economy or shop remain in the active experience.
