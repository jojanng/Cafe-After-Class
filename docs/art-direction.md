# A quiet neighborhood café

Café After Class is an original, unbranded contemporary Korean neighborhood café in miniature. The reference café in Brossard informs the light oak counter, pale stone worktop and warm ribbed pendants. The Roost informs intimacy, Chibi-Robo the tactile proportions, and Spirit City the quiet mood. The geometry and textures are original.

## Fixed interior

Compose a fixed, slightly elevated three-quarter customer view near the service counter. Keep the counter ends, pan, guest pickup and pastry case inside the frame. Widen the lens in portrait to preserve their separation. Extend the plaster and dark brown oak boards to all four edges of the view.

The window wall stays clear of seating. Set glazing behind a deep reveal, slender sash and integrated sill. Slant daylight through the opening, with warm fill from the two pendants. Use indigo enamel (`#2B2C7A`) on the machine and the same indigo for primary controls. Pair it with paper (`#F6F4EE`), warm cream (`#FFF7EA`) and selective white (`#FFFFFF`).

Staff stand behind the counter. The espresso machine's controls, portafilters and drip bay face them, while the viewer sees the outside and end cap of the machine. The counter does not turn. The guest pickup, served glass and projected paper receipt have independent room-space anchors on the customer edge, not within the machine group. Old decoration never changes this composition.

## Calm and responsive service

The study view stays still; the HTML timer updates once per second, without running a continuous scene loop. Motion is a response to a saved action: a 380 ms manual receipt pickup, 420 ms ingredient packet arrival, 700 ms stationary pour and fill, and 560 ms guest pickup slide. Visibility sync, reload and resize reconstruct the saved state rather than replay an effect. Reduced motion shows the final pose immediately.

Place the current task below the scene. A single primary action leads each decision, with the books and 10-second demo accessible from the header. The focus slip accepts an explicit goal and a 25- or 45-minute timer. On expiry the user can collect a receipt and earn a flavor or carry the goal forward. Make consumes ingredients and Serve advances the order. The served slip offers the next ready order, queued session or new goal in that order. Show the queue as a disclosure when it contains goals. Allow the page to scroll for long goals, expanded lists and larger text.
